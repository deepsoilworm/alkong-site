# Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I pair with my partner?
Go to Profile → Connect Partner → either share the code you receive or enter the code your partner sent.

### How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete Account. Your account and all records (questions, answers, partner connection) are deleted immediately.

### I saw inappropriate content.
You can **report** content from the menu on each feed item, and you can **block** the user from their profile page.

### Push notifications aren't arriving.
Check iOS Settings → Alkong → Notifications. Inside the app, Profile → Notification Settings lets you toggle each type.

### How do I cancel my premium subscription?
iOS **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Alkong**. After cancellation you can continue using premium features until the end of the billing period you already paid for.
